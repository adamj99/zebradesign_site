Aisle & Grace · Privacy policy

Your wedding plans stay yours.

Last updated 24 October 2026

The short version
  • Aisle & Grace is a local-first wedding planner — your plans live on your device, not our servers.
  • No analytics, no ads, no profiling, and we never sell your data.
  • Backups are optional, off by default, and go to your own private Google Drive area.
  • Delete the app to remove all local data from your device.

Data we store

  • Local storage. All wedding-planning data (tasks, budget, vendors, guests and timeline) is stored locally on your device in an encrypted app sandbox. Your data lives on your device, not our servers.
  • Optional backups. You can optionally create a private backup to your own Google Drive app-data area. This feature is off by default and only runs when you explicitly choose Backup / Restore in Settings.

What this means for you: we never see your wedding plans, guest lists, budgets or any personal information. It's stored on your device and stays under your complete control.

Data we don't collect

  • No analytics. We don't use analytics SDKs or tracking tools.
  • No ads. We don't show advertisements or collect data for ad targeting.
  • No data selling. We will never sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • No profiling. We don't collect personal information beyond what you choose to store in the app.

Google Sign-In

If you enable Drive backups, we request Google Sign-In to upload and download your encrypted JSON backup to your Drive's hidden appDataFolder. This folder is:

  • Only accessible by Aisle & Grace;
  • Automatically deleted if you uninstall the app;
  • Under your complete control.

You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings, and we respect that immediately.

Your choices

  • Stay local. Keep everything on-device and never sign in to any cloud service.
  • Control backups. Enable or disable backups anytime. Delete backups from Google Drive whenever you want.
  • Revoke access. Remove Google Drive access from your Google Account settings.
  • Complete deletion. Remove the app to delete all local data from your device.

Contact

Questions about privacy? We're here to help — email hello@zebradesigns.app or visit aisleandgrace.com/support.
